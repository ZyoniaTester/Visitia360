Virtual Reality (VR) lets you experience the home as if you were inside it. It’s extremely useful to feel present in a property without traveling.

Augmented Reality (AR) lets you see digitally generated elements while you’re still viewing the real home in front of you. It’s ideal for instantly visualizing renovations or virtual changes in a real property, as well as seeing how it would look furnished.

In other words, Virtual Reality is a fully immersive visit using a VR headset that makes you feel like you’re inside the home, while Augmented Reality allows you to add digital elements to a real space in front of you, showing digital content while still letting you see the real environment.
