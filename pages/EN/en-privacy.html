Section 8

8. User rights

In accordance with the GDPR and LOPDGDD, you have the following rights regarding your personal data:

8.1 Right of Access (Art. 15 GDPR)

The right to obtain confirmation as to whether or not personal data concerning you is being processed, and access to a copy of such data in a readable format.

8.2 Right to Rectification (Art. 16 GDPR)

The right to request correction of inaccurate or incomplete personal data.

8.3 Right to Erasure or “Right to be Forgotten” (Art. 17 GDPR)

You may request deletion of your personal data when:

  • Data is no longer necessary for the purposes collected.
  • Consent has been withdrawn.
  • You object to processing.
  • Data has been unlawfully processed.
  • Deletion is required by legal obligation.

Exceptions: This right shall not apply when processing is necessary for:

  • Compliance with legal obligations.
  • Legal claims or defense.
  • Public interest or official authority.

8.4 Right to Restriction of Processing (Art. 18 GDPR)

You may request restriction of processing when:

  • You contest the accuracy of the data.
  • Processing is unlawful but you oppose erasure.
  • Data is no longer required but needed for legal claims.
  • You have exercised your right to object.

8.5 Right to Data Portability (Art. 20 GDPR)

The right to receive your personal data in a structured, commonly used, machine-readable format and transmit it to another controller where technically feasible.

8.6 Right to Object (Art. 21 GDPR)

You may object to processing, particularly for:

  • Direct marketing and commercial communications.
  • Profiling and automated decision-making.
  • Processing based on legitimate interest.

8.7 Automated Decisions (Art. 22 GDPR)

The right not to be subject to decisions based solely on automated processing that produce legal effects or significantly affect you.
